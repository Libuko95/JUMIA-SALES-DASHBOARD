This repository contains an Excel file (`dashboard.xlsx`) that analyzes product data, focusing on **prices, discounts, reviews, and ratings**.  
The dashboard is designed to help visualize **sales performance** and identify trends between discounts and customer satisfaction.

## 🧩 Contents
- **Key Metrics**  
  - Average current price  
  - Average old price  
  - Average discount percentage  
  - Average rating  

- **Discount Analysis**  
  - Absolute discount by product  
  - Percentage discount categories (Low, Medium, High)  

- **Ratings & Reviews**  
  - Distribution of rating categories (Excellent, Average, Poor)  
  - Scatter plot: Discount % vs Rating  

- **Product Highlights**  
  - Most expensive product  
  - Least expensive product
 
  - - **Interactive Filters**  
  - Discount category  
  - Rating category  
  - Product type  

## 🎯 Purpose
The dashboard helps answer questions such as:
- Do higher discounts lead to more reviews (sales)?  
- Are higher-rated products more expensive or affordable?  
- Which products combine **high discounts with poor ratings**?  

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Open `dashboard.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel.  
2. Navigate to the **Dashboard** sheet.  
3. Use slicers to filter by discount category, rating category, or product type.  
4. Explore charts and KPIs for insights.
